What the site is

Warun shows a computed 0–100 "condition score" for outdoor rock-climbing spots, derived from weather data supplied by Open-Meteo. It's an informational tool, not a safety certification or a guarantee of any kind.

Climb at your own risk

Outdoor rock climbing is inherently dangerous. Weather forecasts and derived scores can be wrong, delayed, or based on a weather station far from the actual crag. A high score is not a promise that a spot is safe to climb, and a low score is not a complete list of hazards. Use your own judgment, check current on-site conditions, and follow local guidance before climbing.
